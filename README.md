# Automated Cartographic Mapping of Africa — Remote Sensing (presentation, in Russian)

Russian-language version of the invited talk on remote-sensing mapping of Africa,
presented at the **Institute of Geography, Russian Academy of Sciences (IG RAS),
Moscow, on 30 October 2025**.

Russian title: «Автоматизированное картографирование Африки: разработка
скриптовых методов обработки ДДЗ для выявления динамики почвенно-растительного
покрова» — *"Automated Cartographic Mapping of Africa: development of scripting
methods for remote-sensing data processing to detect land-cover and vegetation
dynamics."*

This is a Russian translation of the English talk **"Mapping Landscapes of Africa
Using Remote Sensing Data: Detecting Spatio-Temporal Environmental Dynamics from
the Satellite Images"**, originally given at the Department of Geoinformatics,
Paris Lodron University of Salzburg (PLUS), Austria (2024).

The presentation surveys around 45 of the author's published studies on African
environmental and climate dynamics, organised as a country-by-country tour —
Algeria, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad,
DR Congo, Egypt, Ethiopia, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya, Tanzania,
Malawi, Mali (incl. the Inner Niger Delta), Mozambique, Nigeria, Rwanda,
South Sudan, Sudan, Tunisia, Uganda and Zambia — covering satellite image
processing, machine-learning land-cover classification, methodology and data
integration.
